EXPLORE PAKISTAN

Pakistan is a country in South Asia, officially called the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. It was established on 14 August 1947, following the partition of British India. Its capital is Islamabad, while Karachi is its largest city and a major economic center. Pakistan shares borders with India, China, Afghanistan, and Iran, and has a coastline along the Arabian Sea. Pakistan has a population of over 240 million people, making it one of the world's most populous countries. Urdu is the national language and English is widely used officially; Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki, and many other languages are spoken across the country. The country has four provinces: Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan, along with other federally administered territories. Its geography ranges from deserts and fertile plains to some of the world's highest mountains. K2, the world's second-highest mountain, lies in the Karakoram range. Pakistan has a rich cultural heritage influenced by civilizations including the Indus Valley Civilization, as well as Persian, Central Asian, Islamic, and South Asian traditions. Cricket is extremely popular, and Pakistani culture is also well known for its cuisine, poetry, music, textiles, and hospitality.

  7. 🏔️ Passu Cones, Hunza — dramatic mountain peaks and one of the iconic views of northern Pakistan.
