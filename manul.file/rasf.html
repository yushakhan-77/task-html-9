About Albert Einstein

Albert Einstein was a German-born physicist who became one of the most influential scientists of the twentieth century. He is especially famous for developing the theory of relativity and for his contributions to theoretical physics.

His famous equation E = mc² describes the relationship between mass and energy. Einstein received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his explanation of the photoelectric effect.

About Isaac Newton

Sir Isaac Newton was an English mathematician, physicist, astronomer, and author. He is widely recognized as one of the most influential scientists in history.

Newton formulated the laws of motion and universal gravitation. He also made important contributions to mathematics and optics. His book Principia Mathematica is one of the most important works in the history of science.

About Marie Curie

Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist and chemist who conducted pioneering research into radioactivity. She became one of the most famous scientists in the history of science.

Marie Curie discovered the elements polonium and radium with Pierre Curie. She was awarded Nobel Prizes in both Physics and Chemistry, making her a remarkable figure in scientific history.

About Charles Darwin

Charles Darwin was an English naturalist and biologist. He is best known for his contributions to the science of evolution.

Darwin proposed that species change over generations through natural selection. His book On the Origin of Species, published in 1859, had a major impact on biological science.

About Galileo Galilei

Galileo Galilei was an Italian astronomer, physicist, engineer, and mathematician. He played an important role in the development of modern astronomy and experimental science.

Galileo used telescopes to observe objects in the night sky. His observations provided important evidence supporting the heliocentric model, in which Earth and other planets orbit the Sun.
